# 1. Product Vision

Build a world-class online learning ecosystem that goes beyond selling courses.

Instead of simply watching videos like traditional platforms, students should be able to

- Learn through practical projects
- Attend weekly doubt sessions
- Join mentorship programs
- Practice coding
- Build portfolios
- Receive verified certificates
- Apply for internships
- Get placement assistance
- Connect with instructors and industry experts

The platform should become the complete career development ecosystem for students.

---

# 2. Mission

To make industry-level education accessible to everyone through practical learning, mentorship, and career-focused training.

# 12. Product Principles

Every feature should follow these principles:

1. Practical over theoretical.
2. Mobile-first user experience.
3. Fast and intuitive interface.
4. Minimal clicks to complete tasks.
5. AI should assist, not replace learning.
6. Community-driven knowledge sharing.
7. Career-focused outcomes.
8. Scalable, secure, and enterprise-ready architecture.
9. Consistent UI/UX across Web and Mobile.
10. Every feature must provide measurable value to students, instructors, or institutions.
